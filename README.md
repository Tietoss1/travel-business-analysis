Travel Business Performance Analysis
project overview
This profject analyses a travel business dataset using Excel and python to understand ticket sales,destination-level profit and profit margins.

Tools used
Microsoft Excel:formulas, summary tables and charts.
python:data processing and validation
Pandas:grouping, aggregation and report generation.
CSV reports

Key Findings
. London generated the highest total profit at ₦1,350,000.
. Dubai recorded the highest profit margin at approximately 23.08%.
. Accra had the lowest total profit at ₦490,000.
. Total company profit was ₦3,490,000. 
. Total company sales was ₦17,850,000.

Business Recommendations
Investigate destination-level costs, pricing and customer demand before making strategic changes. Analyse more periods of data to determine whether the observed patterns continue over time. Monitor destination profit margins over time.

Skills Demonstrated
- Data validation
- Data aggregation using Pandas
- Excel formulas and charts
- Business reporting
- Data-driven recommendations.

This is a learning portfolio project demonstrating data cleaning checks, business calculations, reportings and analytical communication.
